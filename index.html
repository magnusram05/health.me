Do you care about your health?

If you are ‘busy’ with life that your days end with fatigue and you never bothered to ask ‘why’ then read on.

I know how does it feel to have energy throughout the day and at the end of the day.
I also know the opposite: fatigue throughout and at the end of the day.

The change

  • I started strength training and I started taking vitamin supplements (B-complex and D after routing yearly bloodwork).
  • I never skip breakfast, 5 jars of overnight oats with chia seeds/blueberries/raisins/maple-syrup/soy-milk made and stashed away in the refrigerator, work week is sorted.
  • I pack lunch, making sure I have some green and a source of protein (channa/moong/tofu/panner) crumbled and sauted with mild spices.
  • I eat early dinner, I finish before 7 pm. Sleep at 10 pm. Wake up at 5-5:20 am.

How do I do it?

What do I do after waking up: pranayama and Gayathri Japam. Coffee with biscuit (must cutdown). Scroll phone (must cutdown). Then, do a 20-min strength training using dumbbells, YouTube follow-along routine.

Initially I started off with upper body workout and then moved to full-body, I use 5-pound dumbbell although 8-pound is what the trainer uses in the video.

I feel good after the workout, knowing that I made an effort to take care of my health for myself and for my family.

I intend to continue this. And I want to share this with you all too as I want you all to be fit and healthy.

Solid Plan

A simple guide to your health journey - based on what worked for me.

If you have been thinking and wondering how and where to start, you can try this plan, low-friction plan as you can do this from the comfort of your home, all you need is some intrinsic motivation and a sense of responsibility towards your health and towards your family.

  • Have early dinner and go to bed at 10-10.30 pm
  • Wake up at 5.30 am

This plan starts very gently and takes you smoothly into weight training, preparing you mentally along the way.

Week-1

After getting up

  • 10 pranayamam and 10 gayathri mantram

Week-2

After getting up

  • 10 pranayamam and 10 gayathri mantram
  • 10 thoppu karanams

Week-3

After getting up

  • 10 pranayamam and 10 gayathri mantram
  • 5 thoppu karanams
  • 6 Surya namaskarams

Week-4

After getting up

  • 10 pranayamam and 10 gayathri mantram
  • 5 thoppu karanams
  • 12 Surya namaskarams
🎉 Congratulations — now you are disciplined and ready for the next level 🎉
